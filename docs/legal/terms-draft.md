## 1. About GigoMe

GigoMe (gigome.org) is a free website where employers publish part-time
jobs, gigs and internships in Milan for international students, and students
find them. It is operated by **[LEGAL: operator's name, legal form,
address]** ("we").

**GigoMe is a notice board, not an employment agency.** We don't select
candidates, receive applications, take part in hiring, or act on behalf of
students or employers. Students apply directly to the employer, on the
employer's own page or by email. Any work agreement is only between the
student and the employer. **[LEGAL: check this description keeps GigoMe

## 2. Accounts

- **Students** log in with an email address from a university we work with.
  One account per person.
- **Employers** log in with a work email address and create one company per
  account. A company and each of its jobs are checked by our team before they
  are shown.
- Logins use one-time codes sent by email. Keep access to your email
  account safe: anyone who can read it can log in as you.
- You can delete your account at any time on your account page. You can also
  download your data there (see the privacy notice).

## 4. Rules for students

- Use GigoMe to look for work for yourself. Don't copy jobs or contact
  details in bulk, or use them for anything else.
- Apply honestly; what you send an employer is between you and them.
- Report a job when something seems wrong (the "Report this job" link on
  every job page). Don't send false reports.
- There are daily limits on reports, feedback and saves to prevent abuse.
