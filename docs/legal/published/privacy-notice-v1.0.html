Privacy at a glance

GigoMe enables students to discover work opportunities and reveal contact information or application links supplied by advertisers. Students do not apply through GigoMe. GigoMe does not forward student profiles or applications to advertisers, and advertisers cannot search or view student profiles. Students who choose to contact an advertiser do so independently via email, telephone or an external website.

When a registered student selects Reveal Contact, GigoMe records which student account revealed which listing's contact information and the time of the action. This log is not provided to advertisers and can be viewed only by authorized GigoMe administrators. It is used for the limited purposes described below.

GigoMe uses Supabase for its database and authentication, Cloudflare for hosting and domain-related services, Brevo for authentication emails, and an email inbox service for support and privacy correspondence. The main Supabase project is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany (eu-central-1); other service-provider operations may involve processing elsewhere.

This Notice explains our processing under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and relevant Italian data-protection rules. It should be read together with our Terms and Conditions.

2. Which people and data does this Notice cover?

This Notice concerns visitors, students, business and private advertisers, account representatives and people contacting GigoMe.

Depending on the features actually used and the fields provided, GigoMe may process:

Account and identity information: name or displayed name, email address, account identifier, role (student, business or private advertiser), university affiliation or verified university email where enabled, and account creation/status information.

Student profile information: optional educational background, course of study, languages, skills, past experience, general preferences and, if these fields are enabled, age or nationality. These details are not made available to advertisers through GigoMe. We do not need identity documents, health data or other sensitive information for ordinary profile creation.

Contact-reveal records: student account identifier, listing identifier, timestamp and the technical information reasonably necessary to secure or audit the operation. Only authorized administrators can consult these records. We do not automatically notify advertisers of individual reveals.

Authentication and security information: login email, one-time-code delivery and authentication events, session identifiers or tokens, relevant technical logs and, where necessary, IP address, device/browser data and abuse indicators. We do not ask users to create a GigoMe password, and the normal user profile does not include a stored password or uploaded CV.

Communications and reports: emails sent to hello@gigome.org or privacy@gigome.org, requests for help, complaints, fraud reports and information reasonably necessary to investigate them.

Contract and compliance records: date and version of accepted Terms, relevant account notices and records needed to demonstrate compliance or address a dispute.

Please do not submit information about health, religion, political opinions, children, identity documents, immigration status or other unnecessary sensitive details in free-text fields or ordinary support requests. If such information is received incidentally, we will assess whether it must be restricted or removed.

4. Contact Reveal: exactly what happens?

  1. A registered Student views a Job Listing.
  2. The Student voluntarily selects Reveal Contact.
  3. GigoMe displays the Advertiser's contact information or external application link associated with that listing.
  4. GigoMe records the Student's account identifier, listing identifier and time of the reveal in an administrator-only log.
  5. No student profile, CV, application or reveal log is automatically sent to the Advertiser.
  6. Any subsequent contact or application is initiated by the Student directly outside GigoMe and is governed by the receiving party's applicable privacy information.

The contact-reveal record is personal data even though it is not disclosed to advertisers. It is used only for the limited operational, abuse-prevention and security purposes described in this Notice. We do not use it to create public rankings or sell profiles.

5. Who can access the data?

The account holder can access their own information through the available service features.

Advertisers cannot search, browse or access Student profiles or contact-reveal logs via GigoMe. A Student may choose independently to contact an advertiser outside the Platform.

Authorized GigoMe administrators may access the limited account, listing, reveal and reporting information necessary for their assigned responsibilities, subject to access restrictions and confidentiality safeguards.

Other users and visitors may see listings and the contact information that Advertisers have chosen to display through the relevant service features. We do not intend to publish private Student profiles.

Service providers may process data where necessary to provide the services identified below, in accordance with applicable roles, agreements and privacy requirements.

Authorities and professional advisers may receive information where disclosure is legally required or otherwise permitted and necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

We do not sell Student profiles or disclose them to Advertisers in return for payment.

8. Authentication, local storage and cookies

GigoMe currently uses email-delivered six-digit one-time login codes. Following authentication, information necessary to maintain the login session is stored in the browser's local storage. This technology serves authentication and session continuity; it is not used by GigoMe for behavioural advertising.

According to the current technical configuration, GigoMe does not intentionally deploy non-essential advertising, profiling or analytics cookies. Strictly necessary technical storage and similar technologies can generally be used without prior cookie consent, while transparency remains necessary.

Infrastructure providers may also deploy strictly necessary technical or security mechanisms. If the Platform introduces optional analytics, advertising trackers or other non-essential storage/access technologies, we will revise this Notice and implement the required consent and preference controls before activating them.

10. Account closure and data deletion

Users may request account closure, access, correction or deletion of personal data by writing to privacy@gigome.org. Account deletion is currently processed manually by an authorized administrator; it is not advertised as an automatic self-service function.

We will verify the request to the extent necessary, respond without undue delay and within the applicable GDPR time limits, and delete or restrict relevant personal data when the law requires it, subject to justified retention exceptions.

Closing an account does not automatically remove data a Student independently sent to an Advertiser outside GigoMe; the recipient is responsible for its own processing. It also does not necessarily require deletion of records lawfully retained for security, compliance or legal claims.

12. Children and particularly sensitive information

GigoMe is intended for individuals aged 18 or over. We do not intentionally offer accounts to minors. If we become aware that a minor has registered contrary to these Terms, we will take appropriate steps consistent with applicable law.

We do not require special-category data for ordinary use. Users should not include information concerning health, religion, political opinions, trade-union membership, sexual life or other unnecessary sensitive matters in profiles, listings or support messages.

Private Advertisers and families must not publish names, photographs or identifying details of children or unnecessary household information in job listings.

13. Automated decision-making and profiling

GigoMe does not currently use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on Students, and it does not automatically rank Students for Advertisers or decide who is hired. If the service changes materially, we will assess the applicable legal requirements and update the relevant information before introducing such features.

14. Security and incidents

GigoMe uses access restrictions and authentication to protect information. The technical team reports that Row Level Security is enabled for the project's database tables and that student accounts and contact-reveal records are inaccessible to Advertisers through the intended application permissions. Technical testing and monitoring remain necessary.

Authorized administrators are limited to the information necessary for their responsibilities. While reasonable measures can reduce risk, no internet service is completely risk-free.

If a personal-data breach occurs, GigoMe will assess it, document it and notify the supervisory authority or affected persons where required by GDPR Articles 33 and 34. Reports of suspected vulnerabilities or unauthorized access may be sent to privacy@gigome.org.

15. External applications and third-party services

When a Student follows an external application link, sends an email or calls an Advertiser, those interactions occur outside GigoMe. The external recipient determines its own recruitment procedures and may process information provided directly by the Student.

GigoMe does not automatically receive copies of these applications, the associated CVs, hiring decisions, contracts or payments. Students should consult any applicable privacy notices and avoid disclosing unnecessary personal information when contacting an external party.
