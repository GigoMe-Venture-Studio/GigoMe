Privacy at a glance

GigoMe enables students to discover work opportunities and obtain the application links or email addresses supplied by advertisers. Students do not apply through GigoMe. GigoMe does not forward student profiles or applications to advertisers, and advertisers cannot search or view student profiles. Students who choose to contact an advertiser do so independently via email, telephone or an external website.

When a registered student presses Apply on a listing, GigoMe shows and opens that listing's application link or email address and records which student account pressed Apply on which listing and the time of the action (an apply click). This record is never shown to advertisers: an advertiser sees only how many times students pressed Apply on each of its listings, never who. Each student can see their own apply clicks; otherwise only authorized GigoMe administrators can view them. It is used for the limited purposes described below.

GigoMe uses Supabase for its database and authentication, Cloudflare for hosting, domain-related services, bot protection on the login forms and cookieless visit statistics, Brevo for login-code emails, OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap for maps and address search, and an email inbox service for support and privacy correspondence. The main Supabase project is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany (eu-central-1); other service-provider operations may involve processing elsewhere.

This Notice explains our processing under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and relevant Italian data-protection rules. It should be read together with our Terms and Conditions.

2. Which people and data does this Notice cover?

This Notice concerns visitors, students, business and private advertisers, account representatives and people contacting GigoMe.

Depending on the features actually used and the fields provided, GigoMe may process:

Account and identity information: name, email address, account identifier, role (student or advertiser; GigoMe does not record whether an advertiser is a business or a private individual), the login used to sign up (student or employer), for students the university and how student status was established (an email address of a listed university, or the student's own statement that they are a university student and which university they attend, chosen from a list or typed in), the language used on the site (English or Italian), so that login emails are sent in that language, and account creation, last-login and status information. Student profile information: name, degree level and expected graduation year. These details are not made available to advertisers through GigoMe. We do not need identity documents, health data or other sensitive information for ordinary profile creation.

Advertiser and listing information: the advertiser's name (business or individual), website, description and contact email address, listing descriptions, work location (an area of Milan and, optionally, an exact point on the map), relevant remuneration and contractual information, the application email address and/or external application URL, publication and expiry dates, moderation status and notes, and the number of apply clicks and of students following the advertiser. Published listings and approved advertisers' names, descriptions and websites are visible to everyone; the application email address or URL is shown only to logged-in students who press Apply, and the advertiser's contact email address only to the advertiser and administrators.

Apply-click records: student account identifier, listing identifier and timestamp. Each student can see their own records ("Jobs you opened" on the Saved jobs page, and in "Download my data"); advertisers see only the number of apply clicks on each of their listings; otherwise only authorized administrators can consult these records. We do not notify advertisers of individual apply clicks.

Things students choose to keep: saved jobs, saved searches (a name, the search filters and when the search was last opened, to show how many listings are new) and companies followed. In the service they are visible only to the student; a company sees only how many students follow it, never who. No email is sent about them.

Authentication and security information: login email, one-time-code delivery and authentication events, session identifiers or tokens, relevant technical logs and, where necessary, IP address, device/browser data and abuse indicators. We do not ask users to create a GigoMe password, and the normal user profile does not include a stored password or uploaded CV.

Communications and reports: emails sent to hello@gigome.org or privacy@gigome.org, messages sent with the Feedback button (with the page they were sent from), job reports sent with "Report this job" (the reason and any details), requests for help, complaints, fraud reports and information reasonably necessary to investigate them. Feedback and job reports are stored with the sender's account and read only by authorized administrators.

Contract and compliance records: date and version of accepted Terms (and, for an advertiser, the date the Terms were accepted when its company was created), administrators' moderation decisions on companies, listings and reports (which administrator, when, the decision and any note), relevant account notices and records needed to demonstrate compliance or address a dispute.

Please do not submit information about health, religion, political opinions, children, identity documents, immigration status or other unnecessary sensitive details in free-text fields or ordinary support requests. If such information is received incidentally, we will assess whether it must be restricted or removed.

4. Apply: exactly what happens?

  1. A registered Student views a Job Listing.
  2. The Student voluntarily presses Apply. Visitors who are not logged in are asked to log in first.
  3. GigoMe displays the external application link and/or email address the Advertiser provided for that listing and opens it: the link in a new browser tab or, if there is no link, the Student's email app with a subject line naming the listing.
  4. GigoMe records the Student's account identifier, listing identifier and time of the click. The Student can see their own records; the Advertiser sees only the total number of apply clicks on each of its listings; authorized administrators can see the records.
  5. No student profile, CV, application or apply-click record is automatically sent to the Advertiser.
  6. Any subsequent contact or application is initiated by the Student directly outside GigoMe and is governed by the receiving party's applicable privacy information.

The apply-click record is personal data even though advertisers see only totals. Pressing Apply does not send an application, and GigoMe does not know whether the Student actually applied. It is used only for the limited operational, abuse-prevention and security purposes described in this Notice. We do not use it to create public rankings or sell profiles.

5. Who can access the data?

The account holder can access their own information through the available service features, including the Account page, where "Download my data" provides a copy of the account's data as a file.

Advertisers cannot search, browse or access Student profiles or apply-click records via GigoMe. They see only how many times Students pressed Apply on each of their listings and how many Students follow their company, never who. A Student may choose independently to contact an advertiser outside the Platform.

Authorized GigoMe administrators may access the limited account, listing, apply-click, feedback and reporting information necessary for their assigned responsibilities, subject to access restrictions and confidentiality safeguards.

Other users and visitors may see published listings and approved advertisers' public pages (name, description, website and open listings). The application link or email address of a listing is shown only to logged-in Students who press Apply, and an advertiser's contact email address is never shown to other users. We do not intend to publish private Student profiles.

Service providers may process data where necessary to provide the services identified below, in accordance with applicable roles, agreements and privacy requirements.

Authorities and professional advisers may receive information where disclosure is legally required or otherwise permitted and necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

We do not sell Student profiles or disclose them to Advertisers in return for payment.

8. Authentication, local storage and cookies

GigoMe currently uses email-delivered six-digit one-time login codes. Following authentication, information necessary to maintain the login session is stored in the browser's local storage. Local storage also keeps the language chosen for the site (English or Italian), and, for the open tab only, the browser's session storage keeps the email address a login code was just sent to and whether metro lines are shown on maps. GigoMe itself sets no cookies. These technologies serve authentication, session continuity and the user's own choices; they are not used by GigoMe for behavioural advertising.

According to the current technical configuration, GigoMe does not intentionally deploy non-essential advertising, profiling or analytics cookies. GigoMe uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page visits without cookies or other storage on the visitor's device (section 6). Strictly necessary technical storage and similar technologies can generally be used without prior cookie consent, while transparency remains necessary.

Infrastructure providers may also deploy strictly necessary technical or security mechanisms. If the Platform introduces analytics that use cookies, advertising trackers or other non-essential storage/access technologies, we will revise this Notice and implement the required consent and preference controls before activating them.

10. Account closure and data deletion

Users can delete their account themselves at any time on their Account page (Delete my account). The account and its personal data are deleted immediately, including, for an advertiser, its company, listings and their application contacts, except the records described in section 9 that are kept without the user's account (apply clicks and job reports they sent). On the same page, Download my data provides a copy of the account's data as a file. Users may also request account closure, access, correction or deletion of personal data by writing to privacy@gigome.org, for example if they can no longer log in; such requests are handled manually by an authorized administrator.

A person who signs up through the student login with an email address that does not belong to a listed university, and then answers that they are not a university student, is not given an account: the account created when the login code was requested is deleted at once.

We will verify the request to the extent necessary, respond without undue delay and within the applicable GDPR time limits, and delete or restrict relevant personal data when the law requires it, subject to justified retention exceptions.

Closing an account does not automatically remove data a Student independently sent to an Advertiser outside GigoMe; the recipient is responsible for its own processing. It also does not necessarily require deletion of records lawfully retained for security, compliance or legal claims.

12. Children and particularly sensitive information

GigoMe is intended for individuals aged 18 or over. We do not intentionally offer accounts to minors. If we become aware that a minor has registered contrary to the Terms and Conditions, we will take appropriate steps consistent with applicable law.

We do not require special-category data for ordinary use. Users should not include information concerning health, religion, political opinions, trade-union membership, sexual life or other unnecessary sensitive matters in profiles, listings or support messages.

Private Advertisers and families must not publish names, photographs or identifying details of children or unnecessary household information in job listings.

13. Automated decision-making and profiling

GigoMe does not currently use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on Students, and it does not automatically rank Students for Advertisers or decide who is hired. If the service changes materially, we will assess the applicable legal requirements and update the relevant information before introducing such features.

14. Security and incidents

GigoMe uses access restrictions and authentication to protect information. The technical team reports that Row Level Security is enabled for the project's database tables and that student accounts and individual apply-click records are inaccessible to Advertisers through the intended application permissions. Technical testing and monitoring remain necessary.

Authorized administrators are limited to the information necessary for their responsibilities. While reasonable measures can reduce risk, no internet service is completely risk-free.

If a personal-data breach occurs, GigoMe will assess it, document it and notify the supervisory authority or affected persons where required by GDPR Articles 33 and 34. Reports of suspected vulnerabilities or unauthorized access may be sent to privacy@gigome.org.

15. External applications and third-party services

When a Student follows an external application link, sends an email or calls an Advertiser, those interactions occur outside GigoMe. The external recipient determines its own recruitment procedures and may process information provided directly by the Student.

GigoMe does not automatically receive copies of these applications, the associated CVs, hiring decisions, contracts or payments. Students should consult any applicable privacy notices and avoid disclosing unnecessary personal information when contacting an external party.
