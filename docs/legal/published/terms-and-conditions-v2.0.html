IMPORTANT: How GigoMe actually works

GigoMe is a free online service for discovering job opportunities and obtaining the contact details or external application links supplied by the relevant advertiser. GigoMe does not accept, forward or manage job applications; it does not share student profiles with advertisers. When a student chooses Reveal Contact, GigoMe displays the contact information or external link for that listing. The student then independently decides whether and how to contact or apply to the advertiser outside GigoMe.

To provide, secure and assess this feature, GigoMe records which registered student revealed which listing's contact details and when. This log is accessible only to authorized GigoMe administrators, not to advertisers. Please read the separate Privacy Notice.

Employers and private advertisers are responsible for the accuracy and legality of their listings and for the employment-related obligations applicable to any subsequent engagement. GigoMe is not a party to a work agreement and does not guarantee hiring, remuneration or the conduct of users. These Terms cannot override any legal obligation that applies to GigoMe itself.
