    How it works

    For students

    1. Sign up with your student email and confirm it with a 6-digit code.
    2. Add your languages, availability, experience and skills.
    3. Apply on the employer's site or by email, and track every application in your dashboard.

    For employers

    1. Create an account as a business or as a private person.
    2. Post a role. The team reviews it, usually within one working day.
    3. Students contact you directly. Accept or reject them from your dashboard.

          Making part-time work in Milan accessible in English.

          GigoMe is a small, trusted job board that connects international students in Milan with businesses and people looking for flexible, English-speaking help. It is designed and run by a team of students.

          Why we're building this

          The language barrier. Many international students arrive without fluent Italian. Most part-time listings assume it, so capable students are filtered out before they can apply.

          Scattered roles. English-friendly roles exist, but they are spread across group chats, forums and word of mouth. There is no single trusted place to find them.

          Small businesses can't reach students. Cafés, startups, studios and families want short-term help but have no easy way to reach motivated students. Large career portals are built for corporate recruiting.

          Learning by building. As a student project, this is also how we practise strategy: validating a real problem, designing a solution and testing it with users.

          Principles

          • Student-first. Free for students and built around lecture timetables and exam seasons.
          • Trust-based. Verified students, employers reviewed by the team, and honest reviews from students who did the work.
          • Direct contact. Students apply on the employer's site or by email. Contracts and pay stay between student and employer.
          • Non-profit. No fees and no paid placements.

          Contact

          Questions about a role, an account or working with us? Write to the address for your side and we'll reply within two working days.

            Privacy notice

            Draft. This notice will be checked by a legal adviser before launch.

            What we collect

            • Your student email, only to confirm that you're a student. Employers never see it.
            • Your profile: name, username, contact email, programme, languages, availability and the background fields you choose to fill in.
            • Age and nationality, only if you add them. Employers see them only if you tick "Show to employers".
            • Your activity: the applications you track, the roles and searches you save and the employers you follow.
            • Reviews you write. Anonymous reviews hide your name from employers and other students. The team can still see who wrote them, to deal with abuse.

            Who sees your profile

            Only reviewed employers, and only the parts marked as shown to employers. Your profile is never public on the web and never sold or shared for advertising.

            Employers

            We store the employer profile, contact details and listings you submit. Your area is hidden from students if you turn off "Show my area".

            Your rights

            You can see, download and delete your data at any time from your dashboard, or by writing to us. You can withdraw consent to email alerts at any time.

            This demo

            In this demo, everything you enter stays in your browser's local storage and is never sent to a server. "Reset" in the top bar deletes it.

            Contact

            Questions about your data: contact the team.

            Using GigoMe

            • Student accounts are for students with a verified university email.
            • Employer accounts are for businesses and private people hiring in or around Milan.
            • GigoMe is free for both sides. We never charge fees or take a share of pay.

            Roles

            • Every role must be real, legal and paid, with the pay stated clearly.
            • If a role says "No Italian needed", English must be enough to do it.
            • The team reviews roles before they go live and can remove any role that breaks these rules.

            Reviews

            • Only students who were accepted for a role can review that employer.
            • Reviews must be honest and about the work. We remove insults, personal data and fake reviews.

            Contracts and pay

            GigoMe connects students and employers. It is not a party to any contract, and it doesn't handle pay. Agree the terms of the work directly with each other.
